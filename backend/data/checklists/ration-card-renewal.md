# Ration Card Renewal — Document Checklist

A ration card must be renewed before its expiry date to keep subsidised food grain
entitlements active for the household. Renewal can be filed at the local Food and Civil
Supplies office or through the state portal. Applications submitted after expiry may
attract a late fee and can lead to a temporary suspension of benefits, so renewals should
be started at least thirty days in advance.

The applicant should carry the existing ration card, proof of residence, and a recent
passport-sized photograph of the head of the household. Proof of residence can be an
electricity bill, a water bill, a rental agreement, or a bank passbook showing the current
address. If the applicant's name or address differs from the existing card, an updated
identity proof such as an Aadhaar letter or a voter identity card is also required. All
original documents are to be shown for verification and photocopies are to be submitted
with the form.

After the form is submitted, the office issues an acknowledgement receipt with an
application number. The applicant should retain this receipt until the renewed card is
delivered, which typically takes fifteen to thirty working days. Household members can be
added at the time of renewal by attaching their identity proofs and photographs; removals
require a declaration signed by the head of the household. If the application is rejected,
the receipt includes the reason and the window for filing an appeal.
